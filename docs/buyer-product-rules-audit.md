# TechScout buyer rules audit

25 November 2026 · [Audited revision](https://github.com/han33-dotcom/TechScout/tree/620588743adec1aa94a1f6c4c784a68c58e8d7b9) · [Buyer-flow milestone #76](https://github.com/han33-dotcom/TechScout/issues/76)

The buyer wants to know **which Lebanese seller has the right item, what they would pay, and what still needs checking**. The owner has clarified that eligibility follows the actual seller, the retailer supplies delivery fees, and the agent can show initial offers and continue the conversation to obtain a better quote. This audit records where today's rules and evidence support that promise and where the product contract needs to change.

## One consequence for the acceptance test

In screening case B17, ITWorks documented a Beirut store, the exact new Samsung monitor in stock at **$233.88 including tax**, and **$5 delivery to Tripoli**. The seller-based rule supports **$238.88 delivered** in that dated independent check. The original screening marked it partial because the unit's dispatch origin was unknown. When the contract is adopted, reclassify the reference with the rule version and preserve the original observation. A live TechScout result and a second seller's comparable total each still need their own evidence. [Screening log, case B17](../acceptance/buyer-v1/screening-2026-11-25.json)

## Two choices for the contract review

- **How should a buyer see a promising incomplete offer?** Suppose one shop quotes $745 delivered and a second lists the same laptop at $730 before delivery. The second shop might be cheaper. I recommend showing both together with explicit price labels, reserving the lowest-delivered-price claim until the second quote arrives. Today's UI puts uncertain offers in a separate section. [Comparison UI](buyer-ui.md)
- **How should address sharing work across sellers?** If two shops each need a street address, should the buyer approve them together or one at a time? I recommend naming both shops and letting the buyer select either or both in one step. A later shop would need its own choice. Existing private history can store clarification answers, so the quote design also needs to specify where address data goes and when it expires. [Session API](buyer-api.md) · [History and exports](buyer-history.md)

The next contract review can use one laptop delivery to Tripoli to resolve both choices. Its observable outcome is a comparison the buyer can act on before and after a retailer quote, including when the buyer declines to share more detail.
